Buying & Selling

Experience That Protects Your Investment

A real estate deal moves fast. From the moment an offer is signed, the clock starts on attorney review, inspections, financing, title, and closing, and every step has deadlines and fine print that can cost you real money. In Illinois, the attorney review period is your best chance to fix a bad deal before it becomes a binding one.

That is why it matters to have an attorney who knows the laws, knows the process, and has navigated it for hundreds of transactions. We negotiate the contract in your favor, resolve inspection disputes, make sure the title work is done properly, and untangle the complications that can derail a closing, so yours stays on track.

What We Handle

Contracts, Title, and Complicated Closings

Every transaction we take runs through the same careful hands, contract negotiation and attorney review, earnest money and contingency protection, coordination with your lender, examination of the title commitment, and clearing the liens, survey issues, and defects that surface along the way.

Most closings are routine. The ones that are not, an estate selling an inherited home, a missing heir on title, a seller in foreclosure, a deal collapsing the week of closing, are where experience earns its keep. We have seen it, and we have closed it.
