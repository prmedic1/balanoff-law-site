Privacy Policy

Effective June 12, 2026

Dan Balanoff, Attorney at Law ("the Firm," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to give us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and the contents of any message you send through our contact or client intake forms, or the email address you provide when joining our mailing list. Like most websites, we may also receive basic technical information automatically, such as your browser type and the pages you visit, through standard server logs and analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiries, schedule consultations, send mailing list updates you have requested, and operate and improve this website. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

How We Share Information

We share information only with service providers who help us run this website and process form submissions and email (for example, our form and email providers), and only as needed for them to perform those services, or when we are required to do so by law.

Forms and Confidentiality

Submitting a form on this website does not create an attorney-client relationship. Please do not include sensitive or confidential details about your legal matter in a website form. Save those details for a consultation.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings; the site will continue to work with cookies disabled.

Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you send us, and we keep it only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by our professional obligations.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time by using the unsubscribe option in any message or by contacting us. You may also contact us to ask what information we hold about you or to request that it be corrected or deleted.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time by posting a revised version on this page with a new effective date.
